8. Engineering Recommendations & Quality Gates

  • Strongest Measured Areas: Decoupled MJPEG pipeline throughput (36+ FPS), RT-DETR helmet detection recall on rider crops, and Zero-DCE low-light enhancement contrast gains.
  • Weakest Areas: ANPR OCR exact-match drops under heavy motion blur/night noise without dedicated camera alignment; multi-camera VRAM allocation scales linearly above 8 streams.
  • Recommended Improvements: Integrate CRNN custom plate font fine-tuning dataset, upgrade Kalman motion state update frequency during fast vehicle trajectory changes, and enable OpenVINO fixed shape compilation for Intel iGPU offload.
