9. Architectural Decoupling & Scalability Targets

Decoupled Video & Telemetry Pipeline

CamAI decouples live MJPEG video streaming from AI inference telemetry. The MJPEG video feed runs asynchronously at the camera's native frame rate. AI detection bounding boxes and analytics are overlayed via WebSocket JSON telemetry.

Benefit: Heavy inference loads or temporary processing spikes degrade overlay latency slightly but never block or freeze the live video display.

Scalability Status & Targets

  • ✓Validated Benchmark: Demonstrated multi-stream execution on 1 to 4 concurrent RTSP streams per edge instance.
  • ⚠Large-Scale Deployment Target: Architecture designed for 100–500 camera enterprise installations via multi-edge node clustering.
  • ⚠Validation Requirement: Production scale beyond 4 concurrent streams requires buyer load testing during due diligence.
