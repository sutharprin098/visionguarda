# CamAI (VisionGuarda) - ₹50 Lakhs ($60,000 USD) Payment & Legal Handover Guide

This guide details the exact process for safely receiving a high-value payment (₹50 Lakhs / $60,000 USD) when selling the **CamAI** project IP and codebase without any risk of fraud or non-payment.

---

## 1. Safe Payment Options (Domestic vs International)

### Option A: International Buyer (Global Deal - $60,000 USD via Escrow.com)
*For buyers outside India (US, Europe, Dubai, Singapore, etc.)*

**Platform:** [Escrow.com](https://www.escrow.com/) (The industry standard for software & domain sales).

#### Step-by-Step Escrow.com Flow:
1. **Create Escrow Transaction:** Seller or Buyer creates a new transaction on Escrow.com for $60,000 USD with a 5-day inspection period.
2. **Buyer Deposits Funds:** Buyer wires $60,000 USD directly to Escrow.com.
3. **Escrow Verification:** Escrow.com verifies and secures the funds (Status: "Secured").
4. **Code & Asset Transfer:** Seller transfers GitHub repo ownership (`sutharprin098/visionguarda`), credentials, and documentation to the buyer.
5. **Inspection Period:** Buyer inspects code functionality (5 days).
6. **Funds Release:** Buyer clicks "Accept". Escrow.com wires the full $60,000 directly to Seller’s Indian Bank Account via SWIFT wire transfer.
7. **FIRC Certificate:** Seller receives FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) from their Indian bank for tax compliance.

---

### Option B: Indian Buyer (Domestic Deal - ₹50 Lakhs INR via Bank Escrow / RTGS)
*For Indian buyers, companies, or system integrators*

#### Payment Schedule (Milestone-based):
1. **Token Amount (20% = ₹10,000,000 / ₹10 Lakhs):** Paid upfront via RTGS upon signing the IP Transfer Agreement.
2. **Escrow / Milestone Release (80% = ₹40,000,000 / ₹40 Lakhs):** Held in Bank Escrow / Chartered Accountant Escrow or released upon GitHub repository transfer.

## 2. Essential Legal Documents Required for ₹50 Lakh Deal

Before accepting payment, ensure the following agreements are executed:

1. **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):** Protects source code details during buyer negotiation.
2. **Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (IPAA):** Legally transfers ownership of source code, patents, trademarks, and design rights to the buyer.
3. **Bill of Sale / Invoice:** Official invoice generated for tax filing.

---

## 3. Banking & Tax Compliance Checklist (India)

- [ ] **PAN & Bank Details:** Ensure seller's bank account can receive large RTGS / SWIFT transfers.
- [ ] **LRS / FIRC Certificate:** For international transfers, bank provides FIRC within 48 hours.
- [ ] **GST Compliance:** Software IP transfer qualifies as Supply of Service (GST rate: 18% for domestic, 0% export for international with LUT).
- [ ] **Capital Gains / Business Income Tax:** Consult a Chartered Accountant (CA) to optimize tax liabilities.
