## 🤖 1. AI Prompts for Perplexity, ChatGPT & Gemini Research

Copy and paste these exact prompts into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity to uncover names & email addresses of key decision makers:

### Prompt 1: Finding Specific CTOs & Product Heads by Company
```text
I am researching decision-makers at [COMPANY NAME] (e.g., CP PLUS, Matrix Comsec, Sparsh Surveillance, L&T Smart World, Vehant Technologies). 
Please find:
1. Full Name of the current Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Head of Product, or VP of Engineering.
2. Their official LinkedIn Profile URL.
3. Their company email format pattern (e.g., first.last@company.com or initial.last@company.com).
4. Any public business contact email or press release contact associated with their executive team.
Search recent news, press releases, LinkedIn, and corporate announcements.
```

### Prompt 2: Finding Email Patterns & Direct Corporate Emails
```text
What is the standard corporate email address format for employees at [COMPANY DOMAIN, e.g., cpplusworld.com, matrixcomsec.com, sparshsecuritech.com, lntecc.com]?
Provide the top 3 most common email syntax patterns used by [COMPANY NAME] for their engineering and executive leadership teams, along with verified examples from public filings, patent applications, or press releases.
```

### Prompt 3: Deep Executive Lead Generation Prompt
```text
Act as a Senior B2B Tech M&A Researcher. I am looking to contact decision-makers at CCTV manufacturers, Smart City System Integrators, and Video Analytics companies in India, UAE, Europe, and US.
For each company in this list: [INSERT LIST OF COMPANIES], provide:
- Exact Name of Founder/CTO/VP Engineering
- LinkedIn Profile URL
- Business Email Address (or standard email formula)
- Direct phone/WhatsApp number if available on public records
Format the output as a Markdown table ready for export to CSV.
```

## 🛠️ 3. Free Lead Tools & Email Verification Workflow

Use these free tools to turn names & LinkedIn profiles into **100% Verified Emails**:

### Step 1: Extract Executive Profile & Email Formula
1. **Apollo.io (Free Account — 60 free credits/month):**
   - Search by Company Name (e.g., `Matrix Comsec`) + Job Title (`CTO`, `VP Product`).
   - Click "Access Email" to get their verified business email address.
2. **Hunter.io (Free Domain Search):**
   - Enter domain (e.g., `sparshsecuritech.com`).
   - It instantly shows the **Email Pattern** (e.g., 85% of emails are `first.last@sparshsecuritech.com`) and lists verified executive emails.
3. **Snov.io / Anymail Finder:**
   - Chrome extension that finds the direct email right from their LinkedIn profile page.

### Step 2: Email Verification (Prevent Bounce Rates)
Before sending cold emails, always verify the email address to protect your sender reputation:
- **NeverBounce / ZeroBounce / MillionVerifier:** Upload your email list to verify if the inbox is active and deliverable.
- **Gmail Hover Test:** Paste the guessed email (e.g., `rahul.sharma@cpplusworld.com`) into Gmail's "To" field. If a Google profile photo and Google Chat badge pop up, the email is 100% active and real!
