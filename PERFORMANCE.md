## Benchmark Highlights

- **Sub-50ms Pipeline Latency**: Processing time from frame decode to telemetry delivery is under 50ms across configured hardware backends.
- **25–60 FPS Stream Rendering**: Interleaved ByteTrack prediction cycles allow smooth high framerate telemetry even under multi-camera load.
- **Dynamic Resource Scaling**: Closed-loop GPU Tile Governor keeps GPU utilization between 70% and 90%.
